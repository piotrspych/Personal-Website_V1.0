01 · About

A bit about me

I'm a mechanical engineer who loves the full journey of a product — napkin sketch, CAD model, late-night FEA runs, a prototype that doesn't quite fit, and the final part that does.

My focus areas are machine design, structural analysis, and design for manufacturing. Outside of CAD, I write about what I'm building, what I'm learning, and the mistakes that taught me the most.
